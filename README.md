# Gaadi Khata

A free ledger for your cars and bikes. See every rupee your vehicle has cost, what needs attention, and what is coming up.

Your data stays on your own phone. Nothing is uploaded anywhere. The PDF reader loads a small reader program from the internet the first time you use it, but your PDF itself never leaves the phone.

## What it does

- Add up to three vehicles: a car, a bike or a scooter
- **Home screen:** add a fill-up in seconds (odometer and amount; the price is filled in from your last fill-up), see what needs doing, and check this month's spending. A bar at the bottom moves between Home, Add, Vehicles, Insights and More
- **Premium petrol:** tick it on a fill-up to see what premium costs you over regular, and whether the mileage is better
- Log fuel fill-ups, expenses and odometer readings
- See cost per km, year by year and by category
- **Intelligence:** service and tyre reminders, renewals coming up, and a 12-month cost forecast
- **Questions:** keep track of things you still need to look up, and keep your answers as a record
- **What if:** what your kilometres cost, by year or month, with and without the purchase price, plus a cab or public transport comparison and a new-car calculator
- **Keep or replace:** repairs per km over the years, against a new car, in rupees
- **Monthly budget:** what is left this month, and a warning before a big bill
- **Mileage warning:** flags a fill-up that is well below your usual
- **Edit** any entry you added, instead of removing it and starting again
- **Bills from PDFs:** choose a PDF or paste its text. The first time you add a bill from a shop, fill it in once and the app learns that shop's layout, so the next bill fills in by itself

## Start using it

1. Open the link for this app on your phone (ask whoever shared it).
2. Tap **Start my ledger** and enter your vehicle details.
3. Set the service gap and tyre life. They start with typical values. Check your owner's manual or ask your mechanic.
4. In your browser menu choose **Add to Home Screen**, so it opens like an app.

## Keep your data safe

Entries are saved in your browser on this device only.

- Open the **Add** tab and tap **Download backup file** every few weeks, and always before you change phone or clear browser data.
- Safari can clear saved data if you do not open the site for several weeks, so back up regularly.
- Save each backup file to your phone's **Files** app, or to iCloud Drive, Google Drive or Dropbox. That way the file is kept safe even if the phone is lost or reset.
- To move to a new phone, choose **Restore from file** on the setup screen.

## Host your own copy (free)

1. Fork or copy this repository to your GitHub account.
2. Go to **Settings, then Pages**.
3. Under **Branch**, choose `main` and the `/ (root)` folder, then save.
4. After a minute, GitHub shows your web address.

## Good to know

Service gaps, tyre life and costs are guides, not manufacturer promises. Always follow your owner's manual.
